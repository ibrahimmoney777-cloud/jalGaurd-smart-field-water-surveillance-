02 / THE JALGUARD APPROACH

One platform.
Two distinct detection paths.

JalGuard brings sample handling, preliminary measurement and digital records into a modular field-surveillance concept.

MODULE A◉

Microplastic screening

Capture particles through filtration, image them under controlled illumination, then use computer vision to flag possible candidates.

Why separate the modules? Microplastics are particles; PFAS are chemical contaminants. A camera workflow cannot independently confirm PFAS, and optical appearance alone cannot prove a particle's polymer identity.

03 / FROM SAMPLE TO SIGNAL

A traceable path
from water to action.

Each screening event should preserve the context around a result—how the sample was handled, what checks it passed and whether further confirmation is needed.

01♧

Collect & identify

Assign a sample ID and record source, location and collection time.

↘
02▤

Prepare the sample

Use a controlled workflow: filtration for particle screening or a selected PFAS preparation method.

↘
03◎

Measure & analyse

Capture optical images or read the selected sensing signal; apply the appropriate analysis pipeline.

↘
04✓

Check quality

Review calibration, blanks, image quality and other method-specific quality checks.

↘
05⌖

Record & follow up

Store the result with its confidence, limitations, GPS, timestamp and confirmation status.

04 / DIGITAL SURVEILLANCE

Don't just collect results.
Build a history.

JalGuard's digital layer is designed to connect individual screenings with source-level context. Over time, validated records may help teams spot repeat observations and prioritise follow-up.

  • ✓ QR or unique source ID
  • ✓ GPS and timestamp capture
  • ✓ Offline-first recordkeeping concept
  • ✓ Review history and optional cloud sync
  • ✓ Risk-based follow-up—not automatic safety certification
